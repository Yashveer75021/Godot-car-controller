# Godot 4 Physics-Based Vehicle Simulation

A modular, component-driven vehicle dynamics and physics simulation system built in **Godot 4 (GDScript)**. This project bypasses default arcade physics in favor of raycast suspension, contact-patch tire friction mechanics, realistic drivetrain inertia, and thermodynamic tire modeling.

---

##  Overview of Simulated Systems

### 1. Raycast Suspension & Vehicle Dynamics
* **RayCast3D Wheel Integration:** Custom raycast-based wheel nodes compute instantaneous ground clearance, spring compression, and surface normals.
* **Spring & Damping Model:** Full calculation of spring loads in millimeters and velocity ($mm/s$), incorporating asymmetrical **bump** and **rebound** damping coefficients.
* **Anti-Roll Bar (ARB):** Transfers cross-axle suspension compression deltas to resist chassis body roll during hard cornering.
* **Ackermann Steering Geometry:** Non-linear steering angle offset between inner and outer wheels based on input angle and Ackermann coefficients.
* **Aerodynamics:** Continuous aerodynamic drag forces applied to the rigid body using frontal area, air density ($\rho$), and drag coefficient ($C_d$).
* **Brake System & Distribution:** Disc brake torque calculation using clamping force, pad friction ($\mu$), effective rotor radius, and front/rear brake bias with independent handbrake torque.

---

### 2. Powertrain & Engine Mechanics
* **Torque & Throttle Curves:** Samples baked torque curves across normalized RPM ranges scaled by non-linear throttle response curves.
* **Engine Inertia & Redline Dynamics:** Rotational acceleration derived from net torque ($T_{engine} + T_{clutch}$) against engine rotational inertia (moment), featuring rev-limiter bounces and idle stabilization.
* **BSFC Fuel Burn & Mass Depletion:** Real-time fuel consumption using **Brake Specific Fuel Consumption (BSFC)**, dynamically lowering fuel tank levels and subtracting petrol mass ($0.7489\,\text{kg/L}$) from the RigidBody.
* **Dynamic Audio Engine:** Engine sound stream playback pitched dynamically according to instantaneous RPM.

---

### 3. Clutch & Drivetrain Simulation
* **Two-State Clutch Model (Locked & Slipping):** Evaluates rotational angular velocity differences ($\Delta\omega$) between the flywheel and gearbox shaft.
* **Viscous & Coulomb Friction Blending:** Prevents numerical instability and oscillation through damped torque blending and clutch-kick response.
* **Layout Support:** Native support for:
  * **RWD** (Rear-Wheel Drive)
  * **FWD** (Front-Wheel Drive)
  * **AWD** (All-Wheel Drive with configurable front/rear torque split)
* **Gearbox & Inertia Propagation:** Multi-ratio transmission with reverse gear, final drive multiplication, and dynamic gear inertia scaled by the square of the active gear ratio.
* **Automatic & Manual Shifting:** Torque/RPM-based shifting logic predicting adjacent gear torque output, combined with manual sequential input overrides.

---

### 4. Differentials (Front, Rear, Center)
* **Open Differential:** Dynamic torque division and speed-difference split across drive axles.
* **Locked Spool / 4x4:** Rigidly locks axle angular velocity with consolidated rotational inertia across all connected wheels.
* **Limited-Slip Differential (LSD):**
  * Configurable **Power Ratio** (on-throttle lockup)
  * Configurable **Coast Ratio** (engine-braking lockup)
  * **Preload friction threshold** governing transitions between open, slipping, and locked clutch-pack states.

---

### 5. Advanced Tire & Surface Physics
* **Brush Tire Model:**
  * Computes adhesion and sliding regions across contact patch deflections.
  * Accurately calculates lateral cornering force, longitudinal driving/braking force, and self-aligning torque ($M_z$).
  * Non-linear tire load sensitivity modeling grip reduction under high normal loads.
* **Low-Speed Viscous Damping:** Rubber hysteresis stabilization preventing low-speed zero-division singularities and micro-slip chatter when coming to a complete stop.
* **Thermodynamics & Temperature Cycles:** Tracks tire heating generated by slip velocity and normal force, counterbalanced by ambient convective cooling. Optimal temperature curves directly modulate grip coefficient ($\mu$).
* **Physical Tire Wear:** Wear accumulation curve based on slip magnitude, normal load, and surface abrasion.
* **Multi-Surface Detection:** Raycasts read surface collision groups to alter friction ($\mu$) and rolling resistance factors in real time:
  * **Tarmac** ($\mu = 0.85$, $C_{rr} = 0.01$)
  * **Gravel** ($\mu = 0.60$, $C_{rr} = 0.03$)
  * **Grass** ($\mu = 0.55$, $C_{rr} = 0.025$)
  * **Snow** ($\mu = 0.40$, $C_{rr} = 0.035$)

## Project Architecture

| Script | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `base_Car.gd` | Core vehicle manager; handles user inputs, RigidBody forces, engine cycle, and fuel mass. |
| `wheel_suspension.gd` | Raycast suspension, spring/damper calculations, wheel spin integration, and surface querying. |
| `drivetrain.gd` | Transmission gearing, gear inertia, AWD/FWD/RWD distribution, and differential solver. |
| `clutch.gd` | Friction-plate angular velocity matching, torque transmission, and lock/slip states. |
| `brush_tire_model.gd` | Contact patch shear deflection, brush tire equations, and self-aligning torque. |
| `base_tire_model.gd` | Base tire resource managing wear curves, load sensitivity, and thermodynamics. |
| `*_params.gd` | Resource definitions (`CarParameters`, `DriveTrainParameters`, `DiffParameters`, `WheelSuspensionParameters`). |

---

## Usage & Integration

1. Add a `RigidBody3D` vehicle body node and attach `base_Car.gd`.
2. Add four `RayCast3D` child nodes with `wheel_suspension.gd` pointing downward.
3. Create and assign custom `Resource` files for car, drivetrain, differentials, and wheel parameters in the Godot Inspector.
4. Set up input map actions: `Throttle`, `Brake`, `SteerLeft`, `SteerRight`, `Handbrake`, `Clutch`, `ShiftUp`, and `ShiftDown`.
